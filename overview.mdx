## Logs for everything that happens

Quark has [101 log types](/logs/types) in nine categories:

| Category | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Messages | Deleted and edited messages, bulk deletes, deleted polls, removed reactions, threads |
| Members | Joins and leaves, nicknames, roles given and taken, server avatars, bots added and removed |
| Voice | Joins, leaves and moves, streams and video, server mutes and deafens, stages |
| Modlogs | Bans, unbans, kicks, timeouts and timeouts removed |
| Channels | Channels created, deleted and changed, permission changes, webhooks, followed channels |
| Roles | Roles created, deleted and changed, permission changes |
| Server | Server settings, icon, boosts |
| Actions | Invites, emojis, stickers, soundboard sounds, events, AutoMod rules |
| Quark Events | Changes to Quark's own settings in your server |

## Deleted files

When a message with attachments is deleted, Quark posts the files back into your log channel along with the message, so images and other files are not lost with the message.

## A channel for each kind of log

Send everything to one channel, or give each category its own. You can also switch a category off. With Quark Pro or Pro Lite you can give a single log type its own channel.

See [where logs go](/logs/overview#where-logs-go).

## Ignore what you do not need

Leave out particular channels, channel categories, members, roles, or messages with exact content you choose. Switch individual log types off.

See [options](/logs/options).

## Logs on the web

The log viewer shows your server's logs live, with filters for member, channel, log type and text. You can type a question such as "messages deleted by @someone this week" and Quark turns it into filters. With Quark Pro you get 30 days of searchable history and CSV export.

See [log viewer](/logs/viewer).

## Works with your other bots

When a moderator bans, kicks or times someone out through Wick, YAGPDB, Sapphire or ProBot, Quark shows the moderator who gave the command, not the bot. An optional PluralKit setting stops proxied messages from filling your logs.

See [bot integrations](/logs/integrations).

## A troubleshooter that finds the problem

`/troubleshoot` and the dashboard's troubleshooter check your configuration, Quark's permissions in each log channel and the delivery path, then tell you what is stopping logs and fix what they can in one click.

See [troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).

## Share access without sharing permissions

Give a role or a person the log viewer, or part of the dashboard, without giving them Manage Server in Discord.

See [sharing access](/dashboard/access).

## Your language

Quark's logs and commands are available in 19 languages, translated by the community.

See [languages](/languages/overview).
