To log something after it has happened, Quark has to hold on to it first. A deleted message can only be shown if Quark kept a copy while it existed. This section explains what Quark keeps, for how long, and how you control it.

<Note>The [privacy policy](https://quark.bot/legal/privacy-policy) is the authoritative statement of how Quark handles data. These pages explain the same things from the point of view of someone running a server.</Note>

## What Quark receives from Discord

Once Quark is in your server, it receives the following. Copies of files are only kept once a log channel is set.

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Message text, with embeds, stickers, reactions and the names and sizes of attachments | To show what a message said if it is edited or deleted |
| A copy of each attached file | To re-post the file if the message is deleted |
| Member details: user ID, username, display name, avatar, nickname, roles | To show who a log is about |
| Server activity: voice joins and leaves, member joins and leaves, role and channel changes, moderation actions, audit log entries | These are the logs |
| Avatars, server icons, role icons and similar images | To show before and after when they change |

Quark does not receive direct messages, email addresses, or anything from servers it has not been added to.

## Where it goes

- **Your log channels on Discord.** Once a log is posted, it is a Discord message in your server. Your staff control who can see it, and it stays until you delete it.
- **Quark's own storage**, for a limited time. See [behaviour](/data/behaviour) for how long and [storage](/data/storage) for how it is protected.
- **The log viewer**, which shows the same logs on the web to people you allow. See [log viewer](/logs/viewer).

## Your responsibilities

If you add Quark to a server, tell your members that messages, files and activity are logged, and decide carefully who can see your log channels and who has [dashboard access](/dashboard/access).
