Quark logs nothing until you tell it which channel to post in. Setup is three steps: add the bot, choose a channel, check a log arrives.

## Before you start

You need the **Manage Server** permission in the server to add a bot. To run `/logging` you need **Manage Channels** and **Manage Server**. To open the dashboard you need any one of Administrator, Manage Server, Manage Channels or Manage Webhooks.

## Set up Quark

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Quark to your server">
    Open the [invite link](https://quark.bot/api/invite?source=docs), pick your server and approve the permissions.

    Discord shows the list of permissions Quark asks for, with **Administrator** ticked. Administrator is the simplest choice, because nothing can then be blocked by a channel permission. If you would rather not grant it, untick it and read [permissions](/permissions) for what Quark needs instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where logs go">
    After you approve, Quark opens a setup page for your server. Pick one:

    - **Create #quark-logs and log everything there**. Quark makes one private channel that only your admins can see and sends every log to it. This needs Administrator or Manage Channels. If you unticked Administrator, use a channel you already have.
    - **Use a channel you already have**. Choose a text channel from the list and press **Use this**.

    If you added Quark some other way (from Top.gg or Discord's app directory, for example), you will not see that page. Do one of these instead:

    - In Discord, run `/logging` and pick a channel from **Set logging channel**.
    - On the [dashboard](https://quark.bot/dashboard), open your server, go to **Logging → Channels** and set the main logging channel.

    Either way, this sets your **main log channel**. Every log goes there until you [split them up](/logs/overview#where-logs-go).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that a log arrives">
    Quark posts a confirmation message in the channel you chose. Now send a message anywhere in the server and delete it. A **Message Deleted** log should appear in the log channel within a second or two.

    If nothing appears, run `/troubleshoot`. It checks your setup and tells you what is stopping logs. See [troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Deleting your own message is a fine test, unless you have added yourself or one of your roles to the [ignore rules](/logs/options#ignore-rules). The troubleshooter tells you if you have.</Note>

## What to do next

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="code-branch" title="Send different logs to different channels">
    On the dashboard, go to **Logging → Channels**. Each category (Modlogs, Members, Messages, Voice, Actions, Server, Roles, Channels, Quark Events) has a switch and a channel dropdown. Choose a channel to send that category somewhere else, or turn the switch off to stop logging it. Under them, the two special categories, Files and Age-restricted, can each have their own channel but cannot be switched off.

    You can do the same in Discord with `/logging`: pick a category from **Select a category**. A few names differ there (Quark Events is **Quark Config**, Actions is **Server Actions**), and Files and Age-restricted can only be set on the dashboard.

    See [where logs go](/logs/overview#where-logs-go).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="ghost" title="Stop logging certain channels, members or roles">
    On the dashboard, go to **Logging → Ignore Rules** and add the channels, categories, members or roles you want left out.

    An **executor** is the person who did something. A **target** is the person or thing it was done to. Ignoring a member as an executor hides what they do. Ignoring them as a target hides what is done to them.

    See [options](/logs/options).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="magnifying-glass" title="Read your logs on the web">
    Open **Log Viewer** in the dashboard sidebar. It shows your logs as they happen, with filters for member, channel, log type and text.

    See [log viewer](/logs/viewer).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="user-group" title="Give your moderators access">
    Moderators without Manage Server can still be given the log viewer, or part of the dashboard. Go to **Settings → Dashboard Access**.

    See [sharing access](/dashboard/access).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="message-bot" title="Make Quark work with your other bots">
    Quark shows the real moderator on actions taken through Wick, YAGPDB, Sapphire and ProBot, and has an optional PluralKit setting.

    See [bot integrations](/logs/integrations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="crown" title="Keep message history for longer">
    On the free plan Quark can show the content of a deleted or edited message if it was sent in the last 12 hours. Quark Pro extends that to 30 days. Every plan keeps 30 days of searchable log history on the web, and Quark Pro keeps a year.

    See [subscriptions](/subscriptions/overview).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
