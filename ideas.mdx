## A separate channel for moderation

Send **Modlogs** to their own channel so bans, kicks and timeouts are not buried under message logs. Moderators can then press **Edit Reason** on a modlog to add or correct the reason later.

Dashboard: **Logging → Channels → Modlogs**.

## A private channel for deleted files

Set a **Files** channel that only senior staff can see. Deleted attachments are then posted there as their own log, away from the rest of your message logs.

Dashboard: **Logging → Channels → Files**.

## Keep age-restricted content out of your log channel

A deleted or edited message is logged with what it said. Set an **Age-restricted** log channel, itself age-restricted, and the message logs from your age-restricted channels go there and not to your other log channels.

Dashboard: **Logging → Channels → Special categories → Age-restricted**. See [age-restricted channels](/logs/overview#age-restricted-channels).

## Split busy servers across channels

Discord limits how fast messages can be posted to one channel. In a large server, give **Messages** and **Voice** their own channels. The limit applies per channel, so two channels carry twice as much.

The dashboard warns you on the Channels tab when a channel is over the limit.

## Stop modlog reasons being rewritten later

Set **Modlog reasons** to **Lock after 24 hours**. Moderators can still correct a reason on the day, and after that it stays as written. To freeze one modlog straight away, press its **Lock** button. That lock is permanent.

Dashboard: **Logging → Other → Modlog reasons**. See [modlog reasons](/logs/options#modlog-reasons).

## Hear about it when a staff member is banned

On the **Automations** page, start a rule from the idea **Tell a channel when someone with a role is banned**. Choose your staff role and a channel only admins can see, and a role to ping if you want one. Quark posts there whenever a member with that role is banned, with who did it and the reason.

See [automations](/dashboard/automations).

## Keep bot commands out of the logs

Add your bot-commands channel, or a whole channel category, to **Logging → Ignore Rules**. Add common command messages to **Exact content** so deleting them is not logged.

## Quieter voice logs

If you only care about who was in voice, switch off Stream Start, Stream End, Video Start and Video Stop under **Logging → Individual Log Config**. Joins, leaves and moves that happen close together are already bundled into one log.

## An audit trail for Quark itself

Send **Quark Events** to a channel only admins can see. It records every change to Quark's settings in your server: log channels, ignore rules, automation rules, tags, API tokens, dashboard access and subscription changes, with who made the change.

## Give moderators the log viewer

Moderators often do not have Manage Server. Give their role the **Log viewer** preset under **Settings → Dashboard Access** and they can read and search logs on the web without being able to change anything.

See [sharing access](/dashboard/access).

## Answers to common questions as tags

Create tags for your rules, your appeal process or your FAQ on the dashboard, then post one with `/tags send`. Add the `user` option to mention the member you are answering.

See [tags](/commands/tags).

## Hear about outages and updates

When you set a log channel, Quark's confirmation message in that channel has a button to follow Quark's developer updates. Press it and downtime notices and announcements from the developers are posted in that channel. Quark's reply includes a **Disable** button to stop them. You need Manage Server or Manage Channels to use it.

You can also choose the channel, move it or switch the updates off under **Settings → Status updates** on the [dashboard](/dashboard/overview#settings).

## A colour for each log type

With Quark Pro or Pro Lite, set an **Embed Color** per log type so the logs you care about stand out, for example red for bans and grey for voice.

See [customization](/logs/customization).
