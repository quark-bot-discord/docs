Every log type is available on every plan, and each one can be switched on or off individually on the dashboard under **Logging → Individual Log Config**.

## Reading the table

- **ID** is the number the [API](/api-reference/introduction) and the log viewer's filters use for the log type.
- **Name on the dashboard** is what the dashboard and the [log viewer](/logs/viewer) call it. **Title in Discord** is the heading on the log message itself. The two often differ, for example "Member Join" and "User Joined". Channel logs put the kind of channel first, as in "Text Channel Created".
- **Category** decides which channel the log goes to. See [how logs are routed](/logs/overview#where-logs-go).
- **Needs View Audit Log** means the log is built from Discord's audit log. Without the **View Audit Log** permission it is never sent. See [permissions](/permissions#view-audit-log).
- **Always logged for bots** means the log is sent even when a bot did it or it happened to a bot. Other types follow the [bot rules](/logs/behaviour#actions-by-bots).
