There are two ways to find a log: the log viewer on the web, and Discord's own search in your log channel.

## On the web

The [log viewer](/logs/viewer) filters by member, channel, log type, role, time and text, and can answer questions such as "messages deleted by @someone this week".

It covers the last hour on the free plan and Pro Lite, and 30 days on Quark Pro. For anything older, use Discord's search.

## In Discord

Your log channel keeps every log for as long as the messages are there, so Discord's [search](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000468588-Using-Search) reaches back as far as your logs do.

### Find logs about a member

Most logs about a member have their user ID in the footer, after `ID:`. To find them:

1. Open your log channel and click Discord's search box.
2. Choose the `in:` filter and pick your log channel.
3. Type the member's ID and press Enter.

The search ends up looking like this:

```
in: #quark-logs 301655085954367490
```

A few logs do not carry the member's ID in the footer: bundled logs (Multiple Voice Channel Events, Multiple Reactions Removed, Multiple Thread Member Events), and deletions of messages Quark no longer had stored, which show the message ID. On a modlog the ID is the member who was banned, kicked or timed out, not the moderator.

To get a member's ID:

- Press **User ID** on any log about them. Quark replies with the ID, only to you.
- Or right-click a log that mentions them and choose **Apps → Get IDs**. Quark lists every ID in the log, the moderator's included. See [Get IDs](/commands/behaviour#get-ids).
- Or turn on Developer Mode in Discord and right-click the member. See [Discord's guide](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/206346498-Where-can-I-find-my-User-Server-Message-ID).

### Find a kind of log

Add the title of the log to your search. For example, to find deleted messages from one member:

```
in: #quark-logs 301655085954367490 Message Deleted
```

The title is the heading at the top of the log. The [types table](/logs/types) lists the title of every log type.

Discord decides what its search matches inside embeds, so results can vary. If a search misses a log you know is there, use the log viewer or scroll the channel by date.

Giving each category its own channel also narrows a search. See [where logs go](/logs/overview#where-logs-go).

<Note>Older logs end their footer with a second number after the ID. That was the log type ID. New logs no longer include it, so searching by that number only finds older logs.</Note>
