Quark is free to use. Two paid plans add more.

| Plan | In short |
| --- | --- |
| Free | Every log type, every category channel, ignore rules, live log viewer |
| Quark Pro Lite | Free, plus custom colours and formats, a channel per log type, and the choice to log bot actions |
| Quark Pro | Pro Lite, plus 30 days of message history, 30 days of searchable logs on the web, CSV export, and more |

See the [comparison](/subscriptions/comparison) for the full list.

## Ways to get a plan

| Option | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly or yearly subscription | Quark Pro or Pro Lite. Renews until you cancel |
| Quark Pro Lifetime | One payment. Nothing renews and nothing expires |
| Quark Pro packs | Three or six months of Quark Pro for one payment. No renewal. Buying another pack adds to the end date |
| Gift | Buy a pack or Lifetime for someone else. See [gifts](/subscriptions/manage#gifts) |
| Voting | Vote for Quark on Top.gg to unlock Pro Lite for free, a day at a time. See [voting](/subscriptions/voting) |

Prices, and any trial or introductory offer available to you, are on the [pricing page](https://quark.bot/pricing).

## Getting Started

A plan belongs to **your Discord account**, not to a server. After you buy one, you choose which servers it covers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply your plan to a server">
    After checkout you are taken to a setup page. Choose a server and press **Apply**. You can also do this at any time from your [inventory](https://quark.bot/inventory).

    You need Administrator, Manage Server, Manage Channels or Manage Webhooks in a server to apply a plan to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Quark Pro bot (Quark Pro only, optional)">
    Quark Pro comes with a second bot, **Quark Pro**, which you can run alongside the main bot. Add it from the same setup page, or with the [Pro invite link](https://quark.bot/invite/pro).

    <Warning>Apply your plan to the server **before** you add the Pro bot. The Pro bot checks the server's plan when it joins, and leaves if the server does not have Quark Pro yet.</Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the paid features">
    The paid features are available straight away on the main Quark bot. You do not have to add the Pro bot to get them. Logging of bot actions stays off until you turn it on under **Logging → Ignore Rules**.

    Quark posts a **Server Upgraded** log in your Quark Events channel when the plan is applied.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How many servers

Each plan covers **up to 10 servers** that you choose. Pro Lite earned by redeeming votes covers up to 5.

- A server can only have one plan applied at a time. If someone else's plan is already on a server, they have to remove it before you can apply yours.
- To move your plan, remove a server in your [inventory](https://quark.bot/inventory) and add another. There is no limit on how often you do this.

## The Quark Pro bot

The Pro bot is a separate bot with its own configuration. On the dashboard, switch between the **Quark** and **Quark Pro** tabs to configure each one. Running both gives you two independent sets of logs, so one can act as a backup for the other.

You do not need it. A server with Quark Pro gets every Pro feature on the main bot.

Pro Lite does not include the Pro bot.

## Paying

Subscriptions are paid through Stripe, and tax is added at checkout where it applies. Lifetime, packs and gifts are one-off payments. The pricing page shows the payment methods available for each.

For changing plan, cancelling, failed payments and refunds, see [managing your plan](/subscriptions/manage).
