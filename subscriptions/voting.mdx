A vote for Quark on [Top.gg](https://top.gg/bot/704802632660943089/vote) cast through `/vote`, or through a link on the dashboard, gives two rewards. A vote cast directly on Top.gg only counts towards your own balance.

## For a server: 24 hours of Pro Lite

Run `/vote` in a server and vote through the link it gives you. That server gets **Pro Lite for 24 hours**.

- Any member can do this. No permissions are needed.
- Each further vote adds another 24 hours, up to 7 days ahead.
- Locked Pro Lite options on the dashboard link to the same vote page.

`/vote` also shows how long the server's unlock has left and how many votes its members have cast this month.

## For you: a week of Pro Lite

Every vote also counts towards your own balance. **3 votes** can be exchanged for **7 days of Pro Lite on up to 5 servers** that you choose.

1. Open your [inventory](https://quark.bot/inventory).
2. On the **Pro Lite** tab, press **Redeem Pro Lite!**
3. Choose the servers to apply it to.

A balance of fewer than 3 votes expires 5 days after your most recent vote. A balance of 3 or more does not expire.

<Warning>Vote rewards are Pro Lite, for accounts without a plan. Do not redeem them if you already have a paid plan.</Warning>

## Rules

- You can vote once every 12 hours.
- A vote can take up to a minute to register.
- Top.gg counts weekend votes twice in its own rankings. Quark counts every vote once.

## Vote reminders

Quark thanks you by DM after your first vote in a month and again when you reach 3 votes, and can remind you when you are able to vote again. The DM has buttons to turn reminders on or off, or to stop vote DMs altogether. To turn them back on, run `/vote`.
