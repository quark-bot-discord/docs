## Where things are

| To do this | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| See your plan, the servers that have Pro, and what you pay | [Account → Billing](https://quark.bot/account?tab=billing) |
| See what one server has and what it costs | The server's **Billing** tab on the dashboard |
| Get Pro for another server | **Get Pro for another server** on [Account → Billing](https://quark.bot/account?tab=billing), or the server's **Billing** tab |
| Stop paying for one server | The server's **Billing** tab. See [below](#stop-pro-on-one-server) |
| Move a plan bought before Pro was locked to a server | [Account → Legacy plans](https://quark.bot/account?tab=legacy) |
| Update your card, see invoices, cancel | [Billing](https://quark.bot/subscriptions/billing) |

Billing opens your Stripe customer portal. Lifetime, packs and redeemed gifts are paid once, so there is nothing to cancel. If you paid by card, billing still shows your invoices.

## Change plan

On a monthly subscription, a server's **Billing** tab has **Switch to yearly · 2 months free**. It changes the whole plan to yearly.

On the [pricing page](https://quark.bot/pricing), a subscriber sees **Change Period** on their plan, and a Pro Lite subscriber also sees **Upgrade to Pro**. The change takes effect immediately, and what you have already paid for the current period is taken into account. The page tells you what you will be charged before you confirm.

When you cancel, the cancellation page may offer **Buy Lifetime**. Lifetime then covers the servers your plan covers, each priced by its size, and stays on them. Your subscription is set to end at the close of its paid period and is not charged again.

Packs cannot run alongside a subscription. Cancel the subscription first and buy a pack once it ends. The cancellation page also offers a pack that starts when your paid period ends.

## Stop Pro on one server

On a subscription that covers more than one server, you can stop paying for one of them:

1. Open the server's **Billing** tab on the dashboard.
2. Press **Stop Pro on this server**, then **Stop Pro here**.

- Pro stays on the server until your next renewal. It is then removed, and the server moves to the free plan.
- You are not charged for the server again.
- Your other servers are re-priced at renewal, so the biggest one left is the full-price one.
- Until the renewal, **Keep Pro on this server** undoes it.

Only the person paying for the plan can do this. If it is your only server, the button is **Cancel Pro** instead. See [cancel](#cancel).

## Cancel

Open [billing](https://quark.bot/subscriptions/billing) and cancel in the portal.

You keep everything you have paid for until the end of the current period.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, Quark sends you a DM with a link to retry or pay another way. If you pay yearly, the DM also offers to switch you to monthly instead. If the payment stays unpaid, the subscription is cancelled.

See the [refund policy](https://quark.bot/legal/refunds).

## When a plan ends

- Paid features switch off on the servers the plan covered.
- **Your settings are kept.** Custom colours, formats and per-log channels stay saved but stop being applied. They apply again if the server gets a plan later.
- Quark goes back to keeping message content for 12 hours, and the log viewer goes back to the last hour.
- Tags above the free limit of 25 are not deleted, but you cannot add more.
- [Automation](/dashboard/automations) rules are not deleted. Rules with an advanced condition, rules that change a log's colour on the free plan, and rules beyond the new plan's number, are parked until the server has the plan again.
- The Quark Pro bot leaves the server.
- When a plan is removed from a server, or ends, Quark posts a **Server Downgraded** log in its Quark Events channel. When a server moves between Pro Lite and Quark Pro, the log is **Server Plan Changed**, with the plan before and after.

## Messages from Quark

Quark sends you a DM when a trial or plan starts, when it renews, when you change or cancel it, when a payment fails, and when a subscription ends. Before a trial or an introductory price ends, it tells you the date and the amount of the first full charge.

If your DMs are closed, these are not delivered. The same information is on [your account](https://quark.bot/account?tab=billing) and in the billing portal.

## Gifts

You can give someone Quark Pro for one server: 1 to 12 months, or Lifetime.

### Give a gift

1. On the [pricing page](https://quark.bot/pricing), press **Gift Pro**.
2. Choose **Months of Pro** or Lifetime, and **Their server's size**. The price depends on the size.
3. Optionally enter the recipient's Discord user ID. Only they, or you, can then redeem it, and Quark sends them the link. Leave it empty and anyone with the link can redeem it.
4. Pay. You get a gift link, which is also listed on the **Gifts** tab of [your account](https://quark.bot/account?tab=gifts).

Gift links do not expire.

### Redeem a gift

1. Open the link and sign in with Discord.
2. Choose the server the gift is for.
3. Press **Redeem for** *server*.

Pro is put on that server straight away and stays there. Then press **Add the Pro bot** to add the Pro bot. See [getting started](/subscriptions/overview#getting-started).

Which servers you can choose:

| Rule | If not |
| --- | --- |
| You have Administrator, Manage Server, Manage Channels or Manage Webhooks in it | It is not listed |
| It is no bigger than the size the gift was bought for. A smaller server is fine | "Too big for this gift" |
| Nobody else's plan is on it | "That server already has someone's Pro on it" |
| If you have a pack running, it is the server that pack is for | "Your running pack is for another server" |

A refused redeem leaves the gift unused, so you can try again.

- If you already have a pack running on the server, the gifted months are added to the end. A gift for a smaller server than your pack cannot extend it.
- A gift bought before per-server pricing still asks for a server, and covers up to 10 servers of any size.
- If you have a running subscription, a gifted pack cannot be redeemed until it ends. The link will still work then. A gifted Lifetime can be redeemed straight away. Your subscription then ends at the close of its paid period and is not charged again.
- If your subscription's last renewal payment failed and is still being retried, the gift page says so and offers **End subscription and redeem**. That ends the subscription now, and you are not charged for that renewal. The gift starts the same day. It may ask why you are leaving, which is optional. **Not now** leaves everything as it is.
- If you already have Lifetime, you cannot redeem it. Pass the link on to someone else.
