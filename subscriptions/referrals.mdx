title: Referrals
description: 'Recommend Quark to another server and get credit off your plan'
---

Send another server your referral link. When that server keeps Quark for a week, you get credit off your own plan, and the server gets 20% off its first payment.

Your link, what you have earned and every server you have sent are on the **Referrals** tab of [your account](https://quark.bot/account?tab=referrals). If you can refer from a server, its **Logging** page on the dashboard also has a **Recommend Quark** card with **Copy link**.

## Who can refer

You need Administrator or Manage Server in a server that:

- has **1,000 members or more**, and
- has Quark, and Quark posted logs there in the last 7 days.

If you do not qualify yet, the tab shows your biggest server and how close it is to 1,000 members. Press **Check again** after something changes.

## Your link

Your link is `quark.bot/r/` followed by your code. Share it with another server's admins, anywhere you like. It opens Quark's invite.

A referral is only recorded when the server adds Quark through your link for the first time. A server that has had Quark before does not count, and a server can only be referred once.

## What the new server gets

**20% off the first payment** of a Quark Pro subscription for that server, the first month or the first year, if it is bought within 30 days of adding Quark. It applies once. It does not apply when the person buying is the one who sent the link, or when the referral is turned down because the server is run by the person who sent it or had Quark before. The server's **Logging** page shows the offer and the date it ends.

A promotion code cannot be used on the same checkout.

## What you get

Credit depends on the size of the server you sent, on day 7:

| Server you sent | Credit |
| --- | --- |
| Under 1,000 members | $3 |
| 1,000 to 9,999 members | $6 |
| 10,000 or more members | $18 |

- Credit comes off your next Quark invoice. It is not cash and cannot be moved to another account.
- It is added in your account's own currency, at the same rate as Quark's prices.
- You can earn up to **$60 of credit each calendar quarter**. A referral over the limit still counts in your stats, without credit.
- Unused credit expires **12 months** after it was added.
- Quark sends you a DM when a referral counts.

If you have Quark Pro Lifetime, you have no invoices. Your credit is saved up instead, and every $6 becomes one month of Quark Pro, sent to you by DM as a [gift link](/subscriptions/manage#gifts) for a server under 1,000 members.

## When a referral counts

Each referral is checked 7 days after the server added Quark. Until then, the tab shows **Day 7 on** and the date.

On day 7 it counts if:

| Rule | If not |
| --- | --- |
| Quark is still in the server | Quark was removed before day 7 |
| The server is not owned or run by you | The server is owned or run by you |
| Quark had not been in the server before | Quark had been in this server before |
| The server has at least 10 members | The server had fewer than 10 members on day 7 |
| Quark logged on at least 3 of the first 7 days | Quark logged on fewer than 3 of the first 7 days |
| At least one of your 1,000+ member servers still uses Quark | None of your 1,000+ member servers use Quark now |

The tab shows each server as **Day 7 on**, **Quick check**, **Counted** (or the credit it earned) or **Not counted**, with the reason.

Referrals are checked for abuse. Some are held for a manual check before they count, shown as **Quick check**. Quark can hold or refuse credit for abuse.

## Credit taken back

If a server you sent removes Quark within **30 days** of your credit being added, the part of that credit you have not used yet is taken back. Credit already spent on an invoice is not. For a Lifetime holder, it comes out of the credit saved up, if it has not become a gift yet.
