Quark's translations are managed with Weblate at [languages.quark.bot](https://languages.quark.bot/engage/quark-bot/). You translate in your browser. No code or Git is needed.

## How to contribute

<Steps titleSize="h3">
    <Step title="Create a Weblate account">
        [Register here](https://languages.quark.bot/accounts/register/) and verify your email address.
        <Warning>Your email address is public by default. The next step hides it.</Warning>
    </Step>
    <Step title="Hide your email address">
        In your [profile settings](https://languages.quark.bot/accounts/profile/), change your email to the no-reply address Weblate provides. It looks like `username@users.noreply.languages.quark.bot`.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Start translating">
        Open the [Quark project](https://languages.quark.bot/projects/quark-bot/), choose your language and translate.
        <Tip>If your language is not listed, see [adding a new language](/languages/add-language).</Tip>
    </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules for translations

- **Be accurate.** Translate the meaning, not word for word.
- **Make sense.** Read it back as a native speaker would.
- **Keep the format.** Line breaks, punctuation and Markdown such as `**bold**` should stay as they are.
- **Do not translate anything inside curly brackets.** Text such as `{user}` or `{channel}` is replaced by Quark when the message is sent. Move it to wherever it belongs in your sentence, but leave the text inside the brackets exactly as it is.

## When your translation appears

New translations reach Quark with its next update, so they do not show up immediately. A new language becomes selectable in `/language` once the developers have switched it on.
